# Roadmap, readiness and release scope

[← Wiki home](Home)

ReboundOps is an evolving local developer preview. The roadmap describes *proposals, priorities and gates*, not guarantees or already-shipped functionality.

## Current documented scope

- Linux/macOS local developer preview.
- Native Windows is experimental pending acceptance checks.
- Recovery app, local workspace, evidence review, job evaluation, document preparation and tracking have varying levels of maturity and configuration requirements.
- Hosted multi-user production and broad public beta are **outside** the current release declaration.

## What has to be true before wider release?

Review the documented [release checklist](https://github.com/AnkitParekh007/rebound-ops/blob/main/docs/release-checklist.md), [preview manual QA](https://github.com/AnkitParekh007/rebound-ops/blob/main/docs/preview-manual-qa.md), [security review](https://github.com/AnkitParekh007/rebound-ops/blob/main/docs/preview-security-review.md) and [pilot acceptance](https://github.com/AnkitParekh007/rebound-ops/blob/main/docs/external-pilot-runbook.md). CI results do not replace independent accessibility, usability, privacy and release acceptance.

## Proposed future directions

Improved first-run UX, broader job-source robustness, document preparation, native desktop packaging and community support are possible future work. **Do not represent planned desktop or affiliate features as shipped** unless new release evidence establishes that.
