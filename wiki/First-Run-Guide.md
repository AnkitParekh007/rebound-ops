# Your first ReboundOps workflow

[← Wiki home](Home) · [Installation](Installation)

Use a fictional example first so you can understand the product without uploading real documents or configuring an AI provider.

## 1. Start with your situation

Run `npm start` and open the [local task guide](http://127.0.0.1:4317/#/guide). Choose **Use a fictional example** where offered. Record priorities, available time, practical constraints and the kind of opportunities you are considering.

## 2. Review your evidence

In the full recovery interface, paste excerpts from fictional CV/project notes. Record career achievements, examine exact source quotations, and correct unsupported language. **Confirming a claim records your review; it is not independent third-party verification.**

## 3. Explore possible directions

Compare returning to similar work, shifting to different work, and stepping-stone roles. Check assumptions and missing proof instead of treating any suggestion as a guaranteed outcome.

## 4. Build a realistic seven-day plan

Select a direction, choose actions within your available time and record completed steps or reflections.

## 5. Work through the job search

Use the integrated workspace to configure sources, scan available postings, shortlist jobs, evaluate requirements, prepare CV material and track applications. Review all information before relying on it.

## 6. Back up and revisit

Use the explicit **Download session JSON** or **Download combined career backup** controls. These outputs can include sensitive plaintext; do not share them publicly. Use the application's restore preview before overwriting local workspace records.

## What ReboundOps does not do

It does not promise interviews, job offers or specific hiring probabilities. It does not automatically submit applications. Optional AI functionality and some browser/document exports require additional configuration.
