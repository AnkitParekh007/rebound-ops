# ReboundOps Wiki

**A local-first, open-source career recovery and job-search workspace for software professionals.**

ReboundOps helps you decide which opportunities deserve attention, examine the evidence behind your experience, prepare application materials, and track the work of finding your next role. It is created and maintained by **Ankit Parekh** and distributed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/AnkitParekh007/rebound-ops/blob/main/LICENSE).

> **Release status:** Linux/macOS local developer preview. Native Windows remains experimental pending acceptance. This is not a hosted multi-user production service, and ReboundOps does not submit applications for you.

## Product principles

- **Human decisions first:** Job assessments and career directions are advisory, not hiring probabilities.
- **Evidence over embellishment:** User-confirmed career claims are not independently verified facts.
- **Local first:** Browser UI and API primarily run on localhost; explicit options control session saving and external AI requests.
- **Privacy by design:** Keep real resumes, secrets, source documents and backups out of public repositories.
- **Reproducibility:** Follow the project tests, documentation, and fictional walkthrough before trusting a new release.
