# ReboundOps Community Guidelines

ReboundOps exists to help software professionals navigate career transitions with dignity, accurate information and control over their data. We welcome people of different backgrounds, abilities and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific, especially when giving feedback about someone else's career search.
- Explain evidence and trade-offs; welcome good-faith questions and corrections.
- Protect privacy: redact resumes, contact details, private employer records, interview correspondence, credentials and API keys.
- Respect accessibility needs and assume varied levels of technical familiarity.
- Disclose relevant commercial or affiliate interests; do not post unsolicited promotions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, doxxing, sexualized harassment, spam, impersonation, credential collection, and repeated unwanted contact are not permitted. Do not publish another person's private job-search information, fabricate employment outcomes, or imply that a tool guarantees interviews or employment.

## Where to post

- **Wiki:** Product documentation and frequently asked questions.
- **Discussions:** Usage questions, ideas, community introductions and redacted demos.
- **Issues:** Reproducible bugs and specific implementation tasks.
- **Security:** Report suspected vulnerabilities privately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never in a public thread.

## Moderation and enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove sensitive or off-topic content, lock threads, request revisions, or restrict participation when warranted. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and protect other participants. For urgent exposure of secrets or personal data, remove the content as soon as possible and rotate exposed credentials.

If you experience or observe problematic behavior, contact a maintainer through an existing private contact channel. Do not include secrets or sensitive personal data in public GitHub Issues. Maintainers are volunteers; no specific response-time SLA is promised.

These guidelines apply to project discussions, issues, pull requests, Wiki contributions and other ReboundOps community spaces.
