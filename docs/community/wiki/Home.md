# ReboundOps Wiki

ReboundOps is an open-source, local-first job search assistant. It runs on your own computer, needs no account, and works fully without AI. This Wiki collects guides generated from the repository's documentation; the repository is always the source of truth.

**Latest release:** [v0.2.0 (pre-release)](https://github.com/AnkitParekh007/rebound-ops/releases/tag/v0.2.0)
