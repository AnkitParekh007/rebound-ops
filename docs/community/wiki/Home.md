# ReboundOps Wiki

ReboundOps is an open-source, local-first job search assistant. It runs on your own computer, needs no account, and works fully without AI. The guides in this Wiki are generated from the repository's documentation, and the repository is always the source of truth.

**Latest release:** [v0.2.0 (pre-release)](https://github.com/AnkitParekh007/rebound-ops/releases/tag/v0.2.0)
