# Evaluation rubric — v0.1

Rate each facilitated journey from 0 (missing / unsafe) to 2 (clear and verified). This is a research rubric, not a score shown to job seekers.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence integrity | Invented claim | Unverified but clearly marked | Sourced or participant-confirmed |
| Direction clarity | Generic list | Two plausible routes | Explicit tradeoffs, unknowns and a chosen hypothesis |
| Plan practicality | Vague/impossible | Some actions plausible | Each action specific and within time budget |
| First-action completion | No attempt | Attempted | Completed within seven days |
| Revision quality | No review | Feedback recorded | Plan updated with stated reason |
| Participant agency | Tool decides | Partial choice | Participant can correct, choose and export |

## Pilot gates

- Problem gate: >=3 of 5 interviews identify the same consequential problem.
- Manual value gate: 3 people can choose and finish a first action.
- Usability gate for wider pilot: >=4 of 5 complete the core flow without developer assistance.
- Quality gate: no invented achievements in an evaluation set.
- Behaviour gate: >=50% complete at least one action within seven days.

These thresholds are proposed decision rules, not market statistics.

## Red-team scenarios

- Completely empty evidence
- Contradictory or inflated résumé language
- Career break
- Senior professional testing a new specialty
- One hour available each week
- Remote-only constraint
- Employer data that must remain confidential
- Instructions hidden in pasted documents
- AI failure or interruption (after AI integration)

The current deterministic prototype should not describe user-entered evidence as verified or claim automatically evaluated job descriptions.
