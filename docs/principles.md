# ReboundOps principles

ReboundOps helps experienced software professionals decide what to try next during a career transition. These are product commitments, not a claim to have invented career planning.

1. **Start with the person’s constraints.** Available time, practical needs and uncertainty shape the next action.
2. **Keep a claim separate from its support.** Source quotations, self-reporting, confirmation and missing evidence remain visible. A source quotation is not an independent verification of its truth.
3. **Treat direction as an experiment.** Similar work, a new kind of work and a stepping-stone role are hypotheses to test, not predictions about hiring or income.
4. **Make progress manageable.** Present a useful next step and keep the weekly plan within the person’s stated hours.
5. **Let people control their records.** Explain memory, local files, backups and provider calls at the point of choice. No silent recovery-to-engine synchronization.
6. **Use AI only with an understood purpose.** Core recovery works without a model. Optional recovery review previews its payload and asks for consent per request; suggestions do not silently change evidence.
7. **Be honest about the product’s own evidence.** Engineering checks and fictional demonstrations are not participant outcomes.
8. **Keep local-first boundaries clear.** The public marketing site is not the recovery API. Localhost tooling is not a hosted multi-user service until a separate architecture decision says otherwise.
