# Product brief — Phase 1

## Audience

Experienced software professionals recently laid off, especially frontend, full-stack, QA and engineering leads.

## Job to be done

When a person is unsure how to restart their career, help them inventory documented strengths, compare a few realistic paths and perform one manageable action.

## Outcomes, not features

The MVP should answer: What have I demonstrated? What is still unverified? Which route should I test first? What is one action I can finish this week?

## Routes

- **Similar work to before** (Return): roles similar to demonstrated recent work.
- **A new kind of work** (Shift): adjacent roles, with specific unknown requirements to test.
- **A stepping-stone role** (Bridge): scoped, temporary or contract work.

These are hypotheses, not rankings, earnings predictions or vacancy claims.

## Manual-pilot experience

1. Capture role, work preferences, location, urgency and weekly time budget.
2. Collect achievements as user-entered lines, explicitly unverified.
3. Inspect three route hypotheses and the relevant unknowns.
4. Choose one route; generate a <=7-day action list with transparent time estimates.
5. Mark actions complete and record concrete feedback.
6. Export a portable Markdown snapshot.

The browser prototype intentionally does not automatically interpret a résumé or job advertisement. Those tasks need a validated evidence/extraction design.

## Core decision criteria

Success means meaningful action completed, not a more polished résumé. User corrections, choice rationale and follow-through matter more than likes, stars or invented scores.

## Exclusions

No scraping, mass applications, profile enrichment, income promises, psychological diagnosis or automatic career decisions.
