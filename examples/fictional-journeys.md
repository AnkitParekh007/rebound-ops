# Three fictional recovery journeys

All names, details and outcomes below are **invented product fixtures**, not real participant feedback or evidence of ReboundOps effectiveness.

## Journey A — Return: senior frontend engineer

**Situation:** A senior frontend engineer seeks remote/India-based product work. Available time is six hours weekly; income is needed within three months.

**User-entered claims:** Led a component library migration; reduced CI build times; mentored engineers. These should remain **unverified** until the participant can substantiate each one.

**Hypotheses:** Return to frontend platform; Shift toward AI application engineering; Bridge through frontend consulting.

**Selected test:** Return. Supply two current frontend platform job descriptions, mark verified requirements, and rewrite one achievement in a source-backed case study.

**First action:** Capture the before/after evidence from the migration without revealing employer secrets. Estimated 30 minutes.

**Review prompt:** What evidence was found? Did a real role requirement change the preferred direction?

## Journey B — Shift: QA automation engineer

**Situation:** A fictional QA automation engineer has ten years of test infrastructure experience and wants to evaluate site-reliability-oriented test engineering, with four available hours weekly.

**User-entered claims:** Built stable browser test suites; triaged intermittent CI failures; supported incident review.

**Uncertainty:** A role may require production observability and on-call experience not demonstrated in the fictional résumé.

**Selected test:** Shift. Compare two actual job descriptions and create a small test telemetry dashboard with mock data.

**First action:** Choose one relevant production monitoring requirement and write a 45-minute experimental brief, with a clear stop condition.

**Review prompt:** Is the missing experience learnable with a credible sample, or is Return more realistic right now?

## Journey C — Bridge: engineering lead

**Situation:** A fictional engineering lead needs short-term income and has only three hours weekly due to other commitments.

**User-entered claims:** Coordinated a frontend modernization; reviewed design system adoption; coached engineers through release planning.

**Selected test:** Bridge. Define a narrowly scoped consulting offer: one accessibility or architecture review, with explicit deliverables and no unlimited support.

**First action:** Draft a one-page offer specifying scope, exclusions, assumptions and target client. Estimated 40 minutes.

**Review prompt:** Did anyone express actual interest? Is the work scoped and ethically representable?

## Pilot facilitation notes

These examples are not proof of product-market fit. In actual sessions, users must correct claims, supply current role descriptions, choose their own options, and freely opt out. Record real outcomes privately and do not publish without permission.
