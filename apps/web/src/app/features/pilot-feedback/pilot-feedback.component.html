Do not include your name, contact information, employer identities, confidential work or sensitive personal details. Sharing the downloaded report with a facilitator is a separate decision.

A completed checkbox or self-report is not independent evidence of a job-search outcome. No analytics, recruitment or participant study is performed automatically.
