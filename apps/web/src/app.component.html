A better way forward

Your experience matters.
So does your next step.

Compare career directions using user-controlled evidence. Choose one path to test and get a seven-day plan that respects your time.

What this version does: Structured intake, manual evidence confirmation, route hypotheses, time-budgeted plans, local progress tracking and export. What it does not do: AI analysis, job matching, salary predictions, provider calls or disk storage. The API listens on your machine and forgets sessions on restart.
