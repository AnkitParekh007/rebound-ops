↗ ReboundOps Phase 2 · Local preview

A better way forward

Your experience matters.
So does your next step.

Compare career directions using user-controlled evidence. Choose one path to test and get a seven-day plan that respects your time.

What this version does: Structured intake, manual evidence confirmation, route hypotheses, time-budgeted plans, local progress tracking and export. What it does not do automatically: AI analysis, job matching, salary predictions, external provider calls or disk storage. Optional AI requests require your explicit payload preview and consent. The API listens on your machine and forgets sessions on restart.
